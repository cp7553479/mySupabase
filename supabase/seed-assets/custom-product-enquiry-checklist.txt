LogoPress custom product enquiry checklist

1. Product direction
   - Product name or reference number
   - Intended use and target audience

2. Quantity and timing
   - Required quantity
   - Preferred delivery date
   - Delivery country or region

3. Branding and configuration
   - Logo, artwork or reference files
   - Preferred colour, decoration method and placement
   - Any custom packaging or service requirements

4. Contact details
   - Company name
   - Contact name, email and phone number

This checklist helps LogoPress prepare a focused quotation discussion. Final price and production timing are confirmed after the requirements are reviewed.
